Case study

Shop'n Earn: quality assurance

I test shopnearn.in for Hemdip Marketing Pvt. Ltd.: an e-commerce platform with wallets, referral payouts and two Play Store apps.

How I test

  • A written test plan first, so coverage is decided before anything is clicked.
  • Testing on the live site with write requests blocked, so no real data could change.
  • Before-and-after data comparison to prove nothing moved during testing.

What I covered

  • Storefront, categories and checkout.
  • Wallet payments, refunds and orders.
  • Invoices and tax documents.
  • Referral withdrawals and payouts.
  • A 58-screen admin panel, tested button by button.
  • Legal pages and the Play Store listings.

How I report

Every defect gets a severity, the evidence, the expected result, an owner and a status. I use a four-step scale:

  • S1 Blocker: stops the business or loses money.
  • S2 Major: a key flow is wrong or unsafe.
  • S3 Minor: works, but badly.
  • S4 Cosmetic: polish.

What came out of it

  • A defect log of 60 items, ranked by severity.
  • Developer-ready bug reports, including the cause of wallet orders being auto-cancelled.
  • An audit of a 372-product catalogue, and live stock matched to paper registers.
  • Drafts of the legal pages: Terms, Privacy, Refund and Shipping.

Detailed findings stay confidential to the client.
