Case study

Kalokea

A women's fashion store moved from a slow WooCommerce site to a custom build. I delivered it and I still maintain it.

The problem

The existing WooCommerce store was slow, hard to maintain and limited in what could be customised.

What I built

  • A custom storefront with 20+ routes and a 30-module admin panel for products, inventory, orders and the business.
  • Razorpay and cash-on-delivery checkout.
  • India GST invoicing, with CGST, SGST and IGST worked out per order.
  • Order and inquiry updates by email (Brevo) and WhatsApp, and media handled by Cloudinary.

Moving without losing search rankings

I mapped the old WooCommerce URLs to the new ones with 301 redirects and added structured metadata, so the store kept the search rankings it had already earned.

Quality before handover

I reviewed 387 files against a production-readiness checklist and fixed 5 high-severity issues, including a payment-flow bug and an XSS vector. The written audit went to the client as the handover record.

After launch

I now maintain the live store and keep shipping fixes and features for the client.
