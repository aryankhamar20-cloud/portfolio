Custom business software, dashboards and reporting

Spreadsheets work well until several people need to update the same records, managers need dependable reports, or sensitive figures need access controls. I build custom internal software for those workflows: sales activity, quotations, orders, invoices, dashboards and P&L reporting. The aim is to turn an existing process into a shared system with clear responsibilities and consistent calculations.

The MY SOLAR Power sales and P&L case study shows this kind of work. The system supports two companies, four user roles, server-side margin calculations and an audit log. Its PHP API and PostgreSQL database enforce access rules so the application can restrict sensitive information by role. The case study also explains how business figures were compared with the client's monthly workbooks.

For a similar project, start with a sample of the current workflow using dummy or anonymised data. List the people who enter information, the people who approve it and the reports they use. We should agree how company separation, imports, corrections and totals work before building the screens. Internal client systems can stay private; the public case study does not need to expose your operational records.

WooCommerce to Next.js migration and e-commerce development

A custom store can be useful when the existing WooCommerce setup limits the shopping experience or business workflow. A rebuild also needs careful planning: product and category URLs, checkout behaviour, order handling, payment integration and search metadata all matter. I assess these requirements before recommending a migration and scope the features needed for launch separately from later additions.

For Kalokea's WooCommerce to Next.js rebuild, I developed a custom storefront and admin panel with Razorpay, cash on delivery, inventory and order management, and Indian GST invoicing. The work included old-to-new URL mapping with permanent redirects and structured metadata. I reviewed 387 files before handover, and continue maintaining the live store.

Redirects and metadata help preserve existing search signals, but a migration cannot guarantee unchanged rankings. Before a rebuild, collect the old URL list, important landing pages and available Search Console data. After launch, verify redirects, product pages and checkout, then monitor indexing and organic traffic. We can agree the migration checklist and responsibilities as part of the written scope.

Freelance QA testing for live web platforms

You can also hire me for QA without a development rebuild. I prepare a test plan, check important workflows and document defects with reproduction steps and severity. This is useful before a release, during an independent review, or when a live application has recurring problems that need a clear investigation record.

My Shop'n Earn QA case study covers a storefront and a 58-screen admin panel, including wallets and referral payouts. It describes safe live-site checks, a severity-ranked defect log and developer-ready reports. For any production review, we agree which accounts and actions are allowed before testing; transactions and data changes need a suitable test environment or an explicitly agreed procedure.
